MutualX

隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 11 月 25 日

MutualX 是一款运行于浏览器本地的扩展，用于查看你在 X（Twitter）上谁取关了你、谁未回关。MutualX 不是 X Corp. 的官方产品，与 X Corp. 无关。

读取与保存的数据

点击「扫描」时，MutualX 仅使用你当前已登录的 X 会话读取你自己的关注列表（用户 ID、用户名、昵称、头像、认证状态、粉丝/关注数、回关状态与账号受限标记），并在本机 chrome.storage.local（Safari 对应扩展本地存储）按账号分开保存：

  • 扫描结果、互关率与历史互关基线
  • 你手动标记「忽略」的账号
  • 扩展开关状态与浮窗位置

存储与隐私承诺

  • 纯本地存储：没有服务器，不上传、不共享、不出售任何数据。
  • 零追踪：无统计分析、无第三方追踪器、无广告。
  • 操作均需确认：仅在你主动点击「取关」或「取关选中」并二次确认后，才向 X 发送取消关注请求。

权限说明

权限用途
storage 在浏览器本地保存扫描结果、忽略名单与浮窗设置
访问 x.com、twitter.com 在 X 页面展示悬浮卡片，读取关注列表并执行你确认的取关操作

数据删除

在浏览器扩展管理页卸载 MutualX 后，本地保存的所有数据会随之删除。

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

MutualX is a local browser extension that helps you see who unfollowed you or does not follow you back on X (Twitter). MutualX is an independent utility and is not affiliated with X Corp.

Data Read and Stored

When you click "Scan", MutualX uses only your active logged-in X session to read your own following list (user ID, handle, display name, avatar, verification status, follower/following counts, follow-back status, and account restriction flags), and stores it locally on your device in browser extension storage (chrome.storage.local / Safari extension local storage), separated by account:

  • Scan results, mutual follow ratio, and historical mutual-follow baseline
  • Accounts you manually mark as "Ignored"
  • Extension toggle state and floating card position

Storage and Privacy Commitments

  • 100% Local: No backend servers. Your data is never uploaded, shared, or sold.
  • Zero Tracking: No analytics, no third-party trackers, and no ads.
  • Explicit Confirmation: Unfollow requests are sent to X only after you click "Unfollow" or "Unfollow Selected" and confirm.

Permissions

PermissionPurpose
storage Save scan results, ignore lists, and floating card preferences locally in your browser
Access to x.com and twitter.com Display the floating card on X, read your following list, and perform unfollow actions you confirm

Data Deletion

Uninstalling MutualX from your browser immediately removes all locally stored extension data.
